COMFORT, POSITIONING & MOBILITY

Bedridden & Mobility Care

Home support for people with limited mobility or who spend most of their time in bed. Care arrangements focus on comfort, dignity and the practical assistance identified in the individual care plan.

SERVICES INCLUDED

What this category covers

Discuss the specific support required so that the service scope and arrangement can be clarified.

Bedridden Patient Care

Assistance with agreed daily routines and comfort needs for people who are confined to bed or have very limited mobility.

Stroke & Paralysis Care

Support with daily activities and mobility needs for people living with the effects of stroke or paralysis, following professional guidance.

Mobility & Walking Assistance

Assistance with safe movement, transfers and walking when appropriate to the person’s assessed abilities and care plan.

Transfers and mobility assistance should be planned around an individual assessment. Do not attempt unsafe lifting or movement; seek clinical guidance when a person’s condition changes.

Who may enquire?

People with reduced mobility, people recovering after illness and patients who need help with routine movement or daily activities.
