How to Apply for a JNS Job

Steps for applying to a caregiver, attender or healthcare position with JNS.

1. Read the role description

Review the minimum education, duties and role boundaries. Apply only for a role whose requirements you can meet.

2. Prepare your information

Prepare your name, mobile number, current location, education, relevant experience, languages and preferred availability.

3. Complete the online application form

Enter the requested details, read the Recruitment Privacy Notice and tick the consent box before submitting. Your application details are sent to JNS’s recruitment system for review. The online form does not upload a CV, certificates or other files.

4. Share a CV only through an official channel

If JNS requests a CV or resume, first confirm the request through the official JNS phone number or email address. JNS will tell you how to submit it. Do not send original certificates, passwords, OTPs, bank details or unnecessary identity documents at the first enquiry stage.

5. Wait for direct contact

JNS will review the enquiry and contact candidates when appropriate. An application is not an appointment or guarantee of selection.
