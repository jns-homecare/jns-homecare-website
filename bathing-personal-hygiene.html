About Bathing & Personal Hygiene

Personal-care assistance to help a person maintain comfort, cleanliness and dignity at home.

Who may enquire about this service?

Older adults and people who need help with bathing or everyday hygiene.

What may be discussed?

  • Assistance with agreed bathing or wash routines
  • Grooming and dressing assistance
  • Maintaining privacy and comfort
  • Support with preparing personal-care supplies

Preparing for the discussion

  • Share the home location and preferred dates or schedule.
  • Explain the everyday support being requested and what the family can manage.
  • For clinical services, have current instructions from the treating healthcare team available through an agreed appropriate channel.
  • Identify the family contact and preferred communication method.

Before arranging care

JNS will ask about the location, desired schedule, the support being requested and any relevant care instructions. The family and JNS should agree the service description, responsibilities, staffing arrangement, charges and contact process before the start date.

Important scope and safety information

The routine must be adapted to the person’s mobility, skin condition, preferences and any care instructions. Higher-risk transfers require appropriate assessment and trained assistance.

Frequently asked questions

Can bathing assistance be scheduled?

Yes. The requested time and routine can be discussed, subject to staffing and suitability.

Can all caregivers assist with every transfer?

No. Transfers must match the person’s assessed abilities and the caregiver’s training. Higher-risk needs require a suitable professional plan.

Can family preferences be considered?

Yes. Privacy, bathing preferences, language, supplies and preferred routines can be discussed in advance.
