RECOVERY SUPPORT AT HOME

Wound & Recovery Care

Support for people who need help with wound-care routines or a planned period of recovery after a hospital stay or procedure. The scope of support is based on the clinician’s instructions and the patient’s needs.

SERVICES INCLUDED

What this category covers

Discuss the specific support required so that the service scope and arrangement can be clarified.

Wound Dressing Care

Dressing support according to the wound-care instructions provided by the treating clinician.

Bedsore Dressing Care

Support with prescribed pressure-wound dressing routines and observation, alongside agreed pressure-area care measures.

Post-Operative Care

Home support during recovery after surgery, based on discharge instructions and the agreed care plan.

Post-Hospitalisation Care

Help with the transition from hospital to home, including care coordination and assistance with the routines agreed with the family.

Wound appearance, infection concerns or a change in a patient’s condition should be reviewed by a qualified clinician. This service does not replace follow-up appointments.

Who may enquire?

People recovering from surgery, a hospital admission or a wound-related condition who have been assessed as suitable for support at home.
