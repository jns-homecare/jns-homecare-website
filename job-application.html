Job Application

Apply for a non-clinical caregiver or patient care attender role, or register interest in a relevant healthcare position.

Before you apply

Patient Care Attender / Home Caregiver applicants may apply with a minimum education of 12th pass. This is a non-clinical role. Applicants for nursing positions must meet the relevant recognised qualification and registration requirements.

Do not submit original certificates, bank details, Aadhaar/PAN copies or sensitive personal documents at the first enquiry stage. Do not pay anyone claiming to guarantee a JNS job. Ask JNS to confirm any official recruitment process directly.

CV and documents

This online form collects basic application information only. It does not upload a CV or certificates. JNS may request further information later through an appropriate channel.

Candidate application

Your application details will be stored in JNS’s recruitment system for review. This form does not upload a CV. Do not submit Aadhaar/PAN copies, bank details, original certificates or sensitive personal documents. Read the Recruitment Privacy Notice.
