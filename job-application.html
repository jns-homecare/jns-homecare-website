Job Application

Apply for a non-clinical caregiver or patient care attender role, or register interest in a relevant healthcare position.

Before you apply

Patient Care Attender / Home Caregiver applicants may apply with a minimum education of 12th pass. This is a non-clinical role. Applicants for nursing positions must meet the relevant recognised qualification and registration requirements.

Do not email original certificates, bank details, Aadhaar/PAN copies or sensitive personal documents at the first enquiry stage. Do not pay anyone claiming to guarantee a JNS job. Ask JNS to confirm any official recruitment process directly.

CV attachment

The current form prepares an email draft. Add your CV or resume as an attachment in your email application before sending.

Candidate application

This form does not upload or store a CV. Your email application opens with the details; attach your CV yourself and send it to JNS. Submission is not a job offer.
