Home Nursing & Attender Services

Planned home nursing and non-clinical attendant arrangements, with duties and shift details confirmed before service begins.

Families can discuss the person’s support needs, preferred routine, home location and any written instructions from the treating healthcare team. JNS will clarify which tasks are within the proposed role and confirm availability before accepting an arrangement.

Clinical services and procedures are subject to professional qualifications, authorisation, scope of practice and the treating clinician’s instructions. Non-clinical caregivers and attendants do not perform nursing procedures.
