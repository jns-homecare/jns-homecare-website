Home Nursing & Attender Services

Care arrangements for families who need nursing or patient-attender support at home for an agreed period. The role, responsibilities, hours and staff suitability are clarified before service begins.

SERVICES INCLUDED

What this category covers

Discuss the specific support required so that the service scope and arrangement can be clarified.

Home Nursing Care

Nursing support at home based on the care requirements discussed with the patient, family and treating clinician.

Patient Attender Services

Assistance with agreed non-clinical daily routines, comfort, personal care and communication with family members.

12-Hour Care

A planned 12-hour support arrangement, subject to staff availability and confirmation of the required duties.

24-Hour Care

A planned around-the-clock care arrangement using an appropriate staffing and shift plan, subject to assessment and availability.

An attender’s duties are distinct from clinical nursing tasks. Clinical procedures must only be assigned to appropriately qualified and authorised professionals.

Who may enquire?

Families who need scheduled nursing or attendant support at home during the day, night or across a 24-hour period.
