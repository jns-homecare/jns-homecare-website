Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about JNS home-care enquiries, service scope and recruitment.

Service availability

JNS is based in Korattur, Chennai. Availability for a specific location, service and schedule must be confirmed with the office.

Nursing versus attendant support

Clinical nursing procedures require appropriately qualified and authorised professionals. Attendants and caregivers provide non-clinical daily-living support within their training and assigned duties.

Shift arrangements

12-hour, 24-hour and night-time arrangements can be discussed. Staffing, shift timing, breaks, duties and handovers must be confirmed before acceptance.

Enquiries

Call or email JNS. Website forms prepare an email for the visitor to review and send; they do not currently save submissions in an online database.

Caregiver jobs

Patient Care Attender / Home Caregiver applicants may apply with a minimum education of 12th pass. This eligibility applies only to non-clinical daily-care roles. Nursing roles have separate recognised qualification and registration requirements.

Emergency support

JNS is not an emergency medical service. In an emergency, contact local emergency medical services or the treating healthcare team.

Does JNS guarantee 24-hour availability?

No. 24-hour arrangements are subject to a specific staffing and shift plan and written confirmation.

Can an attender give medicines or do dressing?

Attenders do not perform clinical tasks. Medication administration and wound care must be allocated only to appropriately qualified and authorised professionals where permitted.

Can I apply for a caregiver position after 12th?

Yes, 12th pass applicants may apply for non-clinical Patient Care Attender / Home Caregiver roles, subject to selection and role training.
