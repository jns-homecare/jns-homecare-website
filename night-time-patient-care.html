About Night-Time Patient Care

Overnight home support for a person who needs assistance or reassurance during the night.

Who may enquire about this service?

Families who need agreed night-time support for an older adult or a patient with care needs.

What may be discussed?

  • Support during agreed night-time routines
  • Assistance with comfort and everyday needs within the role assigned
  • Communication of concerns to the nominated family contact
  • Handover notes according to the agreed arrangement

Preparing for the discussion

  • Share the home location and preferred dates or schedule.
  • Explain the everyday support being requested and what the family can manage.
  • For clinical services, have current instructions from the treating healthcare team available through an agreed appropriate channel.
  • Identify the family contact and preferred communication method.

Before arranging care

JNS will ask about the location, desired schedule, the support being requested and any relevant care instructions. The family and JNS should agree the service description, responsibilities, staffing arrangement, charges and contact process before the start date.

Important scope and safety information

Night-time care is not automatically continuous clinical observation or emergency nursing cover. Required clinical monitoring and response arrangements must be assessed and explicitly agreed.

Frequently asked questions

Does night-time care mean continuous clinical monitoring?

No. Overnight attendant support is not automatically clinical observation. Any clinical monitoring must be separately assessed and assigned to an appropriately qualified professional.

Can the family request a particular time window?

Yes. Share the requested start and end time. Staffing, duties and availability need confirmation.

What happens if the patient becomes unwell?

Follow the agreed escalation plan and contact the treating team or emergency services as appropriate.
