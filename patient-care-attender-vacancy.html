Patient Care Attender Vacancy

Patient Care Attender / Home Caregiver · Applications invited. Review the role, eligibility and responsibilities before applying.

Applications invited

Role overview

Patient Care Attender / Home Caregiver is a non-clinical daily-care support role. The actual duties, work area, schedule and terms will be explained during recruitment and confirmed before appointment.

Education / qualification

12th pass or above

Responsibilities

  • Assist with agreed everyday activities and personal-care routines within training.
  • Offer companionship and respectful communication.
  • Support simple mobility only where assessed and trained.
  • Follow the agreed daily routine and report concerns to the assigned contact.
  • Respect the person’s privacy, consent and dignity.

Eligibility and selection

  • Minimum education: 12th pass or above.
  • Ability to communicate respectfully with patients and families.
  • Willingness to follow instructions, schedules and role boundaries.
  • Selection and any role-specific orientation are subject to JNS review.

Role boundaries

This role is non-clinical. It does not include tracheostomy procedures, NG tube feeding procedures, catheter procedures, wound dressing, medication administration, diagnosis or independent clinical monitoring.

Employment details

Compensation, work location, shift pattern, weekly off, reporting structure, training or orientation details and any other terms must be discussed directly with JNS. This page does not promise a salary, shift or appointment.
