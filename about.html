ABOUT JNS

Care with dignity.
Support with purpose.

JNS Home Care and Nursing Services is a Korattur, Chennai-based home care service enterprise focused on nursing support and assistance for people at home.

WHO WE ARE

Supporting care in familiar surroundings

We understand that arranging care at home can involve many questions for patients and families. JNS aims to make the first conversation clear: what support is needed, what schedule is preferred, and what arrangements can be confirmed.

Our service portfolio includes home nursing, recovery support, elderly and personal care, mobility assistance, palliative support and patient-attender arrangements.
