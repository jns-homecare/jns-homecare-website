Organising Support After Hospital Discharge

A planning checklist for families arranging a safe transition from hospital to home.

These articles provide general planning information only. They do not offer diagnosis, treatment or clinical instructions and do not replace advice from a treating healthcare professional.

Ask the treating team

Request the discharge summary, written instructions, follow-up plan and information about symptoms that require prompt medical review.

Clarify nursing needs

Identify which tasks are clinical and who is authorised to perform them. Do not ask a non-clinical caregiver to perform nursing procedures.

Prepare the home

Discuss mobility, transport, access, resting space and any equipment recommended by the treating team.

Agree the support plan

Share the general home-support requirement with JNS and confirm the service scope, schedule, staff and terms before the start date.
