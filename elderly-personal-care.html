ABOUT THIS CATEGORY

Elderly & Personal Care

Families can discuss daily routines, preferences, mobility, meal support and personal-care requirements. The duties, schedule and level of assistance are clarified before an arrangement is confirmed.

Clinical services and procedures are subject to professional qualifications, authorisation, scope of practice and the treating clinician’s instructions. Non-clinical caregivers and attendants do not perform nursing procedures. All photographs are illustrative stock images and do not depict JNS staff or clients.
