Elderly & Personal Care

Practical, respectful assistance for older adults who would benefit from support with daily routines, personal care and companionship in familiar surroundings.

SERVICES INCLUDED

What this category covers

Discuss the specific support required so that the service scope and arrangement can be clarified.

Elderly Care

Individualised day-to-day support that respects the older person’s routines, preferences and dignity.

Companionship

Conversation, social interaction and supportive presence during agreed care hours.

Bathing & Personal Hygiene

Assistance with bathing, grooming and everyday hygiene while respecting privacy and personal preferences.

Diaper Changing & Toileting Assistance

Discreet assistance with toileting routines, continence products and related personal hygiene.

Feeding Assistance

Help with meals and drinking routines in line with the person’s established care instructions.

Personal-care support is planned with the individual and family. Swallowing difficulties, special diets or changes in health should be discussed with the treating clinician.

Who may enquire?

Older adults and people who need help with daily living activities or would benefit from regular companionship.
