Patient Care Attender / Home Caregiver

Minimum education: 12th pass or above.

This is a non-clinical daily-care role. Applicants should be respectful, responsible and willing to follow assigned care instructions and role boundaries.

  • Support with agreed everyday activities
  • Companionship and respectful communication
  • Personal-care assistance within training and assigned duties
  • Basic mobility support only where assessed and trained
  • Report concerns to the assigned supervisor or family contact
