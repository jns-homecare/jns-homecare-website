CLINICAL NURSING SUPPORT

Skilled Home Nursing Care

Nursing support arranged around the individual needs of a person receiving care at home. Requirements, staff availability and the appropriate scope of care are discussed before a service is confirmed.

SERVICES INCLUDED

What this category covers

Discuss the specific support required so that the service scope and arrangement can be clarified.

Tracheostomy Care

Support with prescribed tracheostomy-related care and observation, delivered by an appropriately qualified professional within their scope of practice.

Ryle’s Tube / NG Tube Feeding

Assistance with prescribed nasogastric-tube feeding routines and observation, following the treating clinician’s instructions.

Urinary Catheter Care

Support with catheter hygiene, drainage-bag care and observation for concerns that should be escalated to a clinician.

Medication Administration

Medication support in accordance with a valid prescription, documented instructions and the professional’s authorised scope.

Vital Signs Monitoring

Routine recording of agreed observations such as blood pressure, pulse, temperature and oxygen saturation when included in the care plan.

Diabetic Patient Monitoring

Support with glucose monitoring and routine care instructions provided by the treating clinician.

Clinical procedures are not offered as a substitute for medical diagnosis or treatment. The care plan, staff suitability and availability must be confirmed before booking.

Who may enquire?

People who have a prescribed nursing requirement at home, including patients returning from hospital or living with ongoing care needs.
