JNS HOME CARE AND NURSING SERVICES

Skilled Home Nursing Care

Clinical nursing support at home, arranged according to the patient’s care instructions and the professional scope of the assigned team.

ABOUT THIS CATEGORY

Skilled Home Nursing Care

Families can discuss the person’s support needs, preferred routine, home location and any written instructions from the treating healthcare team. JNS will clarify which tasks are within the proposed role and confirm availability before accepting an arrangement.

Clinical services and procedures are subject to professional qualifications, authorisation, scope of practice and the treating clinician’s instructions. Non-clinical caregivers and attendants do not perform nursing procedures. All photographs are illustrative stock images and do not depict JNS staff or clients.
