Care Plans & Schedules

Discuss flexible home-care schedules including 12-hour, 24-hour, night-time and short-term support.

An arrangement built around the enquiry

The right schedule depends on the person’s daily support needs, clinical plan, family availability and local staffing. The website does not promise that every schedule is available at all times.

Schedule options to discuss

12-hour care: a defined day or night shift, with start and end times confirmed in advance.

24-hour care: a coordinated arrangement with planned shifts, handovers, rest periods and staffing confirmed in writing.

Night-time care: agreed overnight assistance and clear escalation contacts.

Short-term care: temporary support after discharge, during recovery or while family arrangements change.

Ongoing care: a longer arrangement reviewed periodically with the family.

What should be confirmed

Exact hours and dates

Whether the role is clinical nursing or non-clinical attendant care

Tasks included and excluded

Staffing and handover arrangements

Charges, payment schedule and cancellation terms

Family communication and escalation plan
